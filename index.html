QA · Percentages

Definition: Percentage means “per hundred”. For example, 20% means 20 out of every 100, so 20% = 20/100 = 1/5.
Worked example:
20% of 250 = (20/100) × 250 = 50.
Q1. What is 15% of 200?
Correct answer: 30. 15/100 × 200 = 30.
Q2. A number increases from 100 to 120. What is the percentage increase?
Correct answer: 20%. Increase = 20; percentage increase = 20/100 × 100 = 20%.
